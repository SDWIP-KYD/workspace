# Pneumothorax vs Hemothorax

![[Pneumothorax-vs-Hemothorax.jpg]]

## Ringkasan

Kondisi di mana terjadi penumpukan **udara** atau **darah** di **pleural space** (ruang antara paru dan dinding dada) yang mengganggu negative pressure dan bisa menyebabkan paru kolaps.

---

### Pneumothorax (Udara di Pleural Space)

**Jenis:**
- **Open** — Udara masuk dari luka terbuka (sucking chest wound)
- **Closed** — Udara masuk tanpa luka (fraktur tulang rusuk, spontaneous)
- **Tension** — Darurat! Udara terperangkap, tekanan naik → tracheal deviation

**Penyebab:**
- Sucking chest wound (gunshot, stabbing)
- Rib fracture
- Spontaneous (rupture of bleb) — Primary (no lung disease history) vs Secondary (ada history)

**Gejala:**
- Dyspnea
- Absent lung sounds (sisi affected)
- **Hyperresonance** pada perkusi
- Subcutaneous emphysema
- Unequal chest expansion
- Tachycardia & hypoxia

**Treatment:**
- Open → Sterile occlusive dressing taped on **three sides** (prevent tension pneumo)
- Tension → Needle decompression

---

### Hemothorax (Darah di Pleural Space)

**Penyebab:**
- Pulmonary embolism
- Thoracic surgery
- **Penetrating trauma:** Puncture from broken rib, gunshot, stab wound
- **Blunt force trauma:** Car accident, fall

**Gejala:**
- Dyspnea
- Absent lung sounds (sisi affected)
- **Dullness** pada perkusi (beda dengan pneumo!)
- Bloody/frothy sputum
- Hypotension
- Unequal chest expansion
- Tachycardia & hypoxia

---

### Treatment (Keduanya)

**Procedures:**
- **Chest tube insertion** — drain fluid/air, re-expand lung
- **Thoracentesis** — needle ke chest cavity untuk drain (chest tube mungkin dipasang setelah)
- **Thoracotomy** — insisi di chest wall (kasus berat)

**Nursing Interventions:**
- Monitor: vital signs, chest tube site, lung sounds
- Monitor chest tube: air leaks, color & amount, water level/suction
- Encourage cough & deep breath
- Teach to splint area while coughing
- Reposition to promote drainage
- Encourage ambulation (with MD order)
