## Sodium
Deficit (ml saline) = Wt(kg) x 4 x (140 - [Na]) / % saline. To incr serum Na by 0.5mmol/L/hr (max safe rate), infusn rate (ml/hr) = 2 x Wt(kg) / (% saline infused); hours of infusion = 2 x (140 - serum Na). 4ml/kg of X% saline raises serum Na by Xmmol/L.
