## Factor 2

## Factor 7a, recombinant (rFVIIa)
Usually 90mcg/kg IV 2H until haemostasis, then 3-6H. See also factor 9 complex.

## Factor 8 concentrate (vial 200-250u), recombinant ­anti­haemophilic factor (rAHF)
Joint 20u/kg, psoas 30u/kg, cerebral 50u/kg. 2 x dose(u/kg) = % normal activity, eg 35u/kg gives peak level of 70% normal. See also Von Willebrand factor / Factor VII concentrate.

## Factor 8 inhibitor bypassing fraction

## Factor 9
IV infsn (max 2u/kg/min): minor hge 25u/kg daily, joint 40u/kg 12-24H, surgery 50u/kg stat then 30u/kg 12-24H, major surgery 85u/kg stat then 50u/kg 12-24H. Proph: 25-40u/kg x2/wk (trough >1u/dl). See also factor 9 complex.

## Factor 9 complex (Beriplex, Ocatplex)
Factors 2, 7, 9, 10, protein C, protein S: approx 25u/ml. 1ml/kg (INR 2-3.9) 1.4ml/kg (INR 4-6) 2 ml/kg (INR >6) + phytomenadione (if reversing warfarin).

## Factor 9 complex (Prothrombinex-VF)
Factors 2, 9, 10 500u per 20ml vial, antithrombin III 25u/vial, low levels factor 5 and 7. 1ml/kg slow IV daily. Risk of throm­bosis in acute liver failure. Reverse warfarin: 1-2ml/kg + phyto­men­adione (Vit K1) + FFP 5ml/kg.
