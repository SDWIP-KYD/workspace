Croup: 1mg/kg stat and in 12hr; severe 4 mg/kg stat, then 1mg/kg 8H. ITP: 4mg/kg dai­ly. Transpl reject, nephrotic synd: 60mg/m2 (max 80mg) daily, reducing over several months. Physiological: 4-5mg/m2 daily. Eye 0.5%: initially 1drop/eye 2H, then 6-12H. 1mg = hydro­cor­tisone 4mg in glucocorticoid activity, 0.8mg in mineralo­cor­ticoid. See also methylpred­nis­olone, neomycin + prednis­olone, phenyl­ephrine + prednisolone.
